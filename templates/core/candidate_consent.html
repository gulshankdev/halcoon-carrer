Candidate Consent & Resume Handling Policy

Our commitment to ethical resume processing and candidate data sovereignty.

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version: 1.0 (Draft for Review)

1. Scope of Consent

When you submit your resume or complete your candidate profile on HALCON CAREER, you grant explicit consent for our recruitment team to evaluate your credentials, cross-reference your skills with relevant vacancies, and contact you via telephone or email regarding employment opportunities.

2. Secure Document Management

Uploaded candidate resumes (PDF, DOCX, DOC) are encrypted at rest where supported and restricted through role-based access control. Public search engines are barred from indexing candidate resumes.

3. Forwarding to Prospective Employers

Your resume is shared with prospective employers only after preliminary screening or explicit communication about the role and organization involved. We do not blast resumes blindly to unverified entities.

4. Data Retention and Right to Deletion

Candidates maintain full control over their uploaded resumes. You may update your resume at any time from your Candidate Profile or request the complete purging of your account and files by emailing {{ COMPANY_EMAIL }}.

5. Verification and Reference Checks

Prior to extending employment offers, hiring employers may conduct standard academic credential verifications, employment history confirmations, or criminal background checks in accordance with applicable labor regulations.
