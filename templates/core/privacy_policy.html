Privacy Policy

How HALCON CAREER collects, handles, stores, and protects candidate and client data.

Effective Date: October 2026 | Version: 1.0 (Draft for Review)

1. Introduction

HALCON CAREER ("we", "our", or "us"), located at SCO 12–13, 2nd Floor, Phase 11, Mohali, Punjab – 160065, is committed to safeguarding personal information collected from candidates and corporate clients through our recruitment portal.

2. Information We Collect

When you register an account, submit an application, or send an employer inquiry, we may collect:

  • Candidate Data: Full name, email address, telephone number, location, education, employment history, skills, and uploaded resumes/curriculum vitae.
  • Employer Data: Business contact person, corporate entity name, business email, phone number, staffing mandates, and job descriptions.
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, and interaction timestamps necessary for security monitoring and session management.

3. Purpose of Processing

Personal information is utilized strictly to:

  • Facilitate recruitment matching between job seekers and hiring employers.
  • Maintain secure candidate accounts and job application records.
  • Contact candidates regarding interview schedules and hiring developments.
  • Fulfill legal, regulatory, and corporate compliance obligations.

4. Resume Storage & Access Controls

Candidate resumes are stored securely and are not made publicly accessible via unauthenticated or indexable web addresses. Resumes are viewable exclusively by authenticated staff recruiters and the respective candidate account owner.

5. Third-Party Disclosures

Candidate details are presented to prospective employers only in connection with genuine hiring mandates. We do not sell, rent, or trade candidate personal information to third-party marketing entities.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For inquiries regarding personal data deletion or updates, please contact our compliance desk at {{ COMPANY_EMAIL }} or visit our office in Mohali.
